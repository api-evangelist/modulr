London, UK | 14 October 2026: Modulr, the payments automation platform built to scale, today announced it is developing purpose-built AI open models powered by NVIDIA for scaling payment operations. The models run inside Modulr’s growing network of AI agents that already operates across its platform and are part of a wider programme to bring agentic intelligence to payments.
