Modulr, the payments automation platform built to scale, today announced that it has selected Alloy, the AI-powered identity and fraud prevention platform, as a key component in its Risk & Compliance Hub. By using Alloy to orchestrate Know your Customer (KYC) and Know your Business (KYB) verification checks across its international customer base, Modulr aims to streamline the onboarding experience for customers while maintaining strong risk, compliance, and financial crime controls.
