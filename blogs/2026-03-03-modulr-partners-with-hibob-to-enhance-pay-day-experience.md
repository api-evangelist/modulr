The partnership improves payroll accuracy, reliability and confidence Modulr , the payments automation platform built to scale, today announces a new partnership with AI powered HR, Finance and Payroll platform, HiBob . The partnership will see HiBob deploy Modulr’s payments automation directly into its payroll workflow, reducing the administrative burden and improving the pay day experience for customers. Pay day is one of the most critical and high-pressure moments for payroll and finance teams, yet payroll payments remain fragmented and manually intensive.
